Drum samples for the "standard drum sound" track.

Taken from **Virtuosity Drums** by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples
(https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums), released under CC0 1.0.
A few velocity layers per drum were mixed from the "mid", "oh" and close mics,
trimmed and faded; the high tom and second crash are pitch-shifted copies.
